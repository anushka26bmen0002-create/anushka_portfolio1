About Me

Hometown: Hindaun, Rajasthan

My Story

I was born and brought up in the vibrant town of Hindaun in Rajasthan. Growing up in a close-knit environment instilled in me a strong foundation of perseverance, grounded values, and an insatiable curiosity about how things work in the world around us.

Following my passion for mechanics and problem-solving, I stepped into higher education to pursue a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering at JECRC University. Here, I have developed analytical rigor, systems-level thinking, and structural problem-solving abilities.

Alongside core engineering, I discovered my true calling at the intersection of technology and innovation: becoming an AI Generalist. Today, I combine technical discipline with the power of modern artificial intelligence to automate workflows, build rapid prototypes, and tackle challenges creatively.
